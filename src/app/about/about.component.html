About the school

Education that prepares children for marks, manners and modern life.

Gandhi Memorial English High School nurtures students through purposeful teaching, personal attention and a school culture built on respect. Children are encouraged to think clearly, communicate confidently and grow into responsible young citizens.

Along with classroom learning, the school supports sports, cultural programs, science activities, leadership opportunities and value-based development.

Academic foundation

Clear concepts, regular assessment and teacher guidance for steady progress.

Holistic growth

Activities that build confidence, teamwork, creativity and discipline.

Our Vision & Mission

Education that breaks barriers and builds brighter futures.

We believe every child deserves access to quality education, encouragement and the opportunity to build a better future.

Our Vision

A Mumbai where every child, no matter their background, gets quality education and a chance to build a better future for their family.

We envision every child from Mumbai's underserved communities growing into a confident graduate who leads with knowledge, values and purpose.

To create a society where education breaks the cycle of poverty.

Our Mission

To provide good and quality education to children from poor families. We guide students with care and discipline so they score good marks, get admission in good colleges, pursue good degrees and become self-reliant.

We are committed to support parents and light the path for students who are the first in their family to study.

We deliver quality, value-based education to students from economically weaker sections, most of whose parents are illiterate but work hard for their children's future.

We nurture academic excellence, good character and life skills so our students secure admission in reputed colleges, pursue professional degrees and transform their lives.

We partner with parents, support students at every step and ensure no child is left behind because of background.

Our commitment

Every child deserves the opportunity to learn, grow, succeed and become self-reliant.
