School Activities

Our school encourages students to participate in a wide range of activities that develop confidence, teamwork, creativity and leadership.

🏆

Sports

“To be a great champion, you must believe you are the best. If you are not, keep on trying.”

Sports competitions help develop the spirit of positive challenge and sportsmanship among our students and prepare them for life ahead. Our school organizes Annual Sports events and encourages students to actively participate in different sports competitions.

Our students have won medals, certificates and trophies in various sports competitions.

🏠

School Houses

“Strength does not come from physical capacity, it comes from unity.”

The students of the school are divided into four different houses: Red, Green, Blue and Yellow.

Each house is managed by teachers and monitors with a House Captain. Vice Captains and House Prefects assist in conducting day-to-day activities and competitions in a systematic and disciplined manner.

👨‍👩‍👧

Parents Teachers Association

PTA meetings are conducted regularly to enhance and improve the children's learning experience and to discuss the activities of our school.

The association also provides an opportunity to seek suggestions and support from parents. A single parent can contribute greatly towards the development and education of a child.

📚

Students Seminars & Workshops

“A person who never makes a mistake never tried anything new.”

To ensure that our students are well equipped with knowledge to face challenges, our school conducts sessions on special topics including health and hygiene.

Students have participated in activities and sessions covering handwashing, nutrition, oral hygiene, leadership skills, career planning, development and safety.

👩‍🏫

Teacher's In-Service Training & Workshops

“Live as if you were to die tomorrow, learn as if you were to live forever.”

In keeping with the school's policy of continuous upgradation of knowledge and skills, our teachers attend various workshops and seminars organized by the government and other experts.

These programmes help our teachers improve their academic and professional skills and enhance their capabilities.

Programmes Conducted in Our School

“Programmes are not just for enjoyment but for how ideas are formed and evolved.”

Our school organizes a variety of programmes and celebrations throughout the academic year. These programmes provide students with opportunities to express themselves, develop confidence and understand the importance of our national and cultural values.

  • Independence Day Celebration
  • Teachers' Day Celebration
  • Gandhi Jayanti
  • Children's Day
  • Guru Purnima
  • Saraswati Pooja
  • Cultural and educational programmes

Students actively participate in these programmes through speeches, songs, dramas, presentations and other activities.

🚌

Field Trips

Field trips provide students with an opportunity to explore new places, experience learning beyond the classroom and develop memorable experiences with their classmates and teachers.

Over the years, our students have visited destinations including:

Matheran Mahabaleshwar Dahanu Water Kingdom EsselWorld Uttan Lonavala Alibaug Tiku Ji Ni Wadi Suraj Water Park Wet'n Joy Water Park

During the 2025–26 academic year, the school organized a field trip to Lonavala's Wet'n Joy Water Park for students of Grades V to XII, accompanied by teaching staff. Primary students also visited Tiku Ji Ni Wadi and Suraj Water Park.

🤝

Social Obligations

“It is not enough to be compassionate, you must act for it.”

As an institution, we believe in extending ourselves towards the upliftment and welfare of society. Our school encourages students and members of the school community to understand the importance of compassion, service and social responsibility.

The school has organized initiatives such as eye check-up camps, computer classes for parents and fundraising activities to support social causes and our soldiers.

🙏

Thanksgiving

“We make a living by what we get, but we make a life by what we give.”

We are grateful to the individuals, organizations and well-wishers who have extended their support towards our students and institution. Their generosity and encouragement have helped us continue our educational and social initiatives.

We sincerely acknowledge the contributions and support received from our donors and well-wishers, including Tata Power, Rotary Club, Karan Gupta Foundation and other supporters.

We remain thankful to everyone whose efforts and generosity contribute to the growth and development of our school community.
