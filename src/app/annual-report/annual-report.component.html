Academic

Academic excellence is one of the important objectives of our school. We encourage students to develop a strong foundation of knowledge, understand concepts clearly and achieve their full potential.

Our teachers provide continuous guidance and support to students throughout the academic year. Various academic activities are conducted to improve learning, understanding and overall performance.

Academic Excellence Encouraging students to learn, achieve and grow with confidence.

Scholarship

Scholarships provide encouragement and support to deserving students and motivate them to continue their academic journey with dedication.

Our school encourages students to make the best use of scholarship opportunities and recognizes their hard work, academic performance and achievements.

Students are encouraged to participate in various competitions throughout the academic year. These activities provide opportunities to discover and develop their talents beyond the classroom.

Art & Drawing

Art and drawing activities provide students with an opportunity to express their imagination, creativity and ideas in an enjoyable and meaningful way.

Students are encouraged to participate in drawing, colouring and other creative activities. These activities help develop observation, imagination, patience and artistic skills.
